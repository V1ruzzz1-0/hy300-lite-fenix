# Hidden Factory Test Menu #
User: v1ruzzz1_0
Date: 10/12/2026
Status: Documenting

While exploring the original Android of the HY300 (RK3326/clone), I discovered a hidden Factory Test menu that can be accessed through the system settings.

# How to Access

1. Open "Settings".
2. Go to "About".
3. Find "Kernel version".
4. Tap that option 7 to 8 times until the test menu appears.

# Options Found

- Version Test: shows the Android version, kernel version, product version (which displays the release keys), Wi-Fi MAC, Ethernet MAC, and an ID.
- Information: shows CPU information, including core count and clock speed, RAM size, and the total space made available by Android.
- LCD: screen color test.
- Touch: touch test (which, for obvious reasons, doesn't work).
- Speaker: audio tests for the left and right channels. The menu describes a song with a male voice playing on the left and one with a female voice on the right, and apparently the songs shown are: Left: terry lin single love song. Right: muay nee ka.
- GSensor: gyroscope test.
- MSensor: motion sensor test.
- Wifi: lists the Wi-Fi networks found.
- SD Card: SD card test, even though my projector doesn't have a slot for that kind of card (and I imagine most don't either).
- Brightness: brightness test.
- storage: shows storage information.
- VideoPlay: plays a video of a futuristic hummingbird, accompanied by a voice saying "welcome to whole new world".

I also noticed that the settings interface provided by the manufacturer is quite limited and doesn't give direct access to all of Android's settings options (such as Developer Options).

However, I found a way to open the system's native settings:

# How to Access

1. Open Settings.
2. Go to Others.
3. Open Apps.
4. Find the app called Settings, which has an icon that looks like the Android 7.1.2 settings.
5. Select the app and tap Open.

After that, Android's native settings become accessible, including the options that don't appear in the manufacturer's limited interface.
