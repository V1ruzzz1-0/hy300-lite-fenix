# New Naming for the Project #
User: v1ruzzz1_0
Date: 10/12/2026
Status: Defined

Until now, I referred to this projector as "HY300 (RK3326/clone)" because I hadn't defined a name of its own to identify it during my research.

I decided to change that.

# Internal Codename (Development): fenix

From now on, this projector's internal codename will be "fenix".

The idea is to have an identifier of its own to make it easier to refer to the device while documenting, organizing files, and doing future research on its hardware and software.

And the name fenix comes from the fact that the hardware is being revived (because with its current software, the device is almost unusable due to the hardware's severe limitations).

# Public Name: HY300 Lite

I also decided to adopt HY300 Lite as the public name to refer to this model.

Even though I'm using the name HY300 as a reference, this device is not the same product sold by Magcubic (which has an Allwinner H713 chipset). But given its physical appearance, software interface, and market naming, I prefer to call it HY300 (for the connection, not a literal kinship) and Lite (because it's weaker than the official one) rather than create a completely new name. And the idea is that even with the new name, I'll sometimes still refer to "HY300 (RK3326/clone)" to stay backward compatible with the documentation prior to this one and to make searching easier.

So this renaming is more about avoiding confusion (it would keep me from installing incompatible things, since this model frequently causes confusion about its internal hardware) than about personalization.

So from now on:

- fenix: the device's internal codename.
- HY300 Lite
- HY300 (RK3326/clone): general reference to the market name and the internal hardware (to tell it apart from the models with the Allwinner H713 chipset).

These names are conventions I adopted to organize the project. They do not represent an official designation from the manufacturer.
